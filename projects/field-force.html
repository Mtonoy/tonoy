Background

Why it existed, and how it was solved

Every engagement in this portfolio started the same way — a business process that had outgrown the tools it ran on.

!The challenge

  • Field officers worked from memory and paper diaries — no proof a visit happened, and no idea why a territory was underperforming.
  • Sales managers spent the morning chasing status updates instead of coaching the team.
  • New leads discovered in the field were lost or double-visited because nothing shared the picture centrally.
  • Target achievement was measured month-end, too late to act within a selling cycle.

✓The solution

  • A daily visit plan that auto-generates from territory targets, then adjusts when an officer misses a slot.
  • Geo-tagged check-in/check-out with a configurable visit radius, so attendance is proven rather than reported.
  • A live command centre showing who is on-site, who is travelling and who is falling behind target.
  • Instant lead capture and reorder from the field, syncing to the sales and inventory systems the moment a device reconnects.

Scope

Modules delivered

What the system actually did for the people using it every day.

Territory Planning

Division, district, territory and outlet hierarchy with capacity and distance-aware assignment.

Visit Scheduling

Auto-generated daily plans from targets, drag-to-reschedule, and carry-forward of missed slots.

Mobile Companion

Offline-first Android client for check-in, order capture, stock count and proof of visit.

Leads & Reorders

Lead capture with duplicates detection, and reorder suggestions from outlet sell-through data.

Performance Scoring

Attainment, visit frequency, order value and territory penetration per officer and per manager.

Sync & Offline

Conflict-safe delta sync that survives patchy field connectivity without losing a single record.

Manager Coaching

Call and check-in logs, feedback notes and rank boards to target coaching where it matters.

Dashboards & Alerts

Exception alerts for missed targets, unplanned absence and sudden outlet churn.
