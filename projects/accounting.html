Background

Why it existed, and how it was solved

Every engagement in this portfolio started the same way — a business process that had outgrown the tools it ran on.

!The challenge

  • Books were maintained in Excel, so the ledger and the bank statement drifted apart and reconciliation was painful.
  • The VAT return was assembled by hand every period, taking days and carrying transcription risk.
  • No enforced double-entry meant misstatements slipped through unnoticed for months.
  • Auditors asked for a trail of who changed what, and when — something spreadsheets could not provide.

✓The solution

  • A strict double-entry engine where every posting is balanced and unbalanced journals are impossible to save.
  • A chart of accounts with NRB-friendly ledgers and automatic VAT treatment on sales and purchases.
  • The statutory VAT return is generated and schema-validated from the ledger — no re-keying.
  • An immutable audit log recording every entry, edit, reversal and approval with user and timestamp.

Scope

Modules delivered

What the system actually did for the people using it every day.

Double-Entry Engine

Balanced journal enforcement, automatic reversals, multi-currency posting and period locking.

Chart of Accounts

Hierarchical ledgers, cost centres, NRB-aligned heads and prior-year comparatives.

Vouchers & Journal

JV, PV, CV, Dr/Cr and sales/purchase vouchers with approval workflow.

Bank Reconciliation

Auto-matching of statement lines to vouchers with one-click manual override and unmatched tracking.

VAT & Tax

Input/output VAT, VAT returns, TIN validation and statutory filing history.

Payables & Receivables

Ageing analysis, due dates, credit limits, follow-up lists and settlement tracking.

Inventory Accounting

Weighted-average and FIFO valuation with automatic GRN and dispatch postings.

Financial Statements

Trial balance, P&L, balance sheet and cash flow generated on demand for any period.
